# Large Account Management & RFP Process Skill

A structured, ready-to-use framework for enterprise sales teams, account managers, and bid professionals. Drop this skill into any AI assistant (Claude, GPT, etc.) or use it directly as a reference playbook — it turns a complex 82-slide enterprise sales methodology into a single, actionable document.

---

## What This Skill Covers

| Module | What You Get |
|--------|-------------|
| **Account Prioritization** | Attractiveness + Ability-to-Win scoring matrices with 7-point scales |
| **Account Classification** | Platinum / Gold / Silver / Bronze tiering with pursuit team assignments |
| **Sweet Spot Identification** | 3-step heat map process to find new opportunities within existing accounts |
| **Client Engagement Model** | Forum cadence, Value Discovery Session template, interaction log |
| **People & Governance** | RAPID decision framework, authority matrix, executive sponsor assignments |
| **RFP Bid Excellence** | End-to-end 4-component process: Qualify → Plan → Solution → Defend |
| **Bid Team Structures** | Roles and responsibilities for Platinum/Gold and Silver/Bronze deals |
| **Pre-Defined SLAs** | Day-by-day milestone timelines for 6–8 week and 3-week RFP cycles |
| **Win Strategy Templates** | Hot buttons, hygiene factors, discriminators, competitor counters |
| **Blue / Pink / Red Reviews** | Full agenda guides for each review stage |
| **Complete RFP Checklist** | Every checkpoint across all four bid components |
| **Performance Management** | Initiative tracking, training plans, organizational enablement |
| **Adaptation Guide** | Step-by-step instructions to tailor the framework to your org and sector |

---

## Who This Is For

- **Account Managers** managing large enterprise relationships and looking to grow revenue systematically
- **Sales Leaders** building a structured bid pursuit process for the first time
- **Bid Managers** running RFP responses and needing a repeatable framework
- **Pre-Sales / Solution Teams** who need to integrate win strategy into their solutioning process
- **Revenue Operations** teams standardizing how their org qualifies and pursues large deals

Works across: IT Services, Consulting, SaaS, Engineering Services, Professional Services, and any B2B sector with complex, multi-stakeholder deals.

## How to Use

### Option A — As an AI Skill (Recommended)

Paste the contents of `SKILL.md` into your AI assistant's system prompt or custom instructions. Then ask it things like:

- *"Score these 5 accounts using the attractiveness and ability-to-win matrix"*
- *"Help me build a win strategy for this RFP — here are the client's hot buttons"*
- *"What should my bid team look like for a $30M deal?"*
- *"Walk me through the Blue Team review agenda for tomorrow's session"*
- *"Generate a proposal summary framework for this opportunity"*

The AI will apply the full framework to your specific situation.

### Option B — As a Reference Playbook

Open `SKILL.md` directly and navigate using the 19-part structure. Each part is self-contained — jump to what you need.

### Option C — Adapt to Your Organization

Follow **Part 19** of the skill to:
1. Replace the illustrative deal tier thresholds with your actual numbers
2. Pre-load your ICP's top hot buttons
3. Map the governance roles to your org chart
4. Set your review cadence by deal size
5. Build your proof point library
