# Large Account Management & RFP Process Skill

A structured, ready-to-use framework for enterprise sales teams, account managers, and bid
professionals. Drop this skill into any AI assistant (Claude, GPT, etc.) or use it directly
as a reference playbook — it turns a complex 82-slide enterprise sales methodology into a
single, actionable document.

**Version:** 1.1.0 — [See changelog](#changelog)

---

## Who This Is For

- **Account Managers** managing large enterprise relationships and looking to grow revenue systematically
- **Sales Leaders** building a structured bid pursuit process for the first time
- **Bid Managers** running RFP responses and needing a repeatable, governed framework
- **Pre-Sales / Solution Teams** who need to integrate win strategy into their solutioning process
- **Revenue Operations** teams standardizing how their org qualifies and pursues large deals

Works across: IT Services, Consulting, SaaS, Engineering Services, Professional Services,
Healthcare, Financial Services, and any B2B sector with complex multi-stakeholder deals.

## How to Use

### Option A — As an AI Skill (Recommended)

Paste the contents of `SKILL.md` into your AI assistant's system prompt or custom instructions.
Then ask it things like:

- *"Score these 5 accounts using the attractiveness and ability-to-win matrix"*
- *"Help me build a win strategy for this RFP — here are the client's hot buttons"*
- *"What should my bid team look like for a $30M deal?"*
- *"Walk me through the Blue Team review agenda for tomorrow's session"*
- *"Generate a filled Opportunity Analysis for this pursuit"*
- *"Which accounts should I prioritize this quarter and why?"*

The AI will apply the full framework to your specific situation.

### Option B — As a Reference Playbook

Open `SKILL.md` directly and navigate using the Quick Reference table at the end.
Each of the 19 parts is self-contained — jump to what you need.

### Option C — Adapt to Your Organization

Follow **Part 19** of the skill:
1. Replace the illustrative deal tier thresholds with your actual numbers
2. Pre-load your buyers' top hot buttons by sector
3. Map the governance roles to your org chart
4. Set your review cadence by deal size
5. Build your proof point library before the first live use
