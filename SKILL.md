# Large Account Management & RFP Process Skill

## Overview

This skill encodes the complete Large Account Management and RFP excellence framework from an
82-slide enterprise sales training deck. It covers four integrated workstreams:

1. **Account Prioritization** — Scoring and tiering accounts by attractiveness and ability to win
2. **Account Planning** — Sweet-spot identification, engagement models, and people/governance
3. **RFP / Bid Excellence** — Four-component bid process from qualification through defense
4. **Performance Management** — Tracking, incentives, and capability-building

Use this skill to structure account plans, qualify and score bids, build win strategies, define
bid teams, and run RFP response processes for enterprise IT services deals.

---

## Part 1: Strategic Shift — From Hunting to Farming

The foundational shift in enterprise account management:

| Past | Present |
|------|---------|
| Net-New (NN) focus | Existing + Expand (EE & EN) focus |
| Hunting | Farming |
| Ad hoc target setting | Account-based planning |
| Ad hoc roles | Competency-based roles |

**Target growth rate for EN accounts: 30%+**

## Part 2: Account Management Framework

Good account management requires doing two things well:

```
Account Prioritization → Account Planning → Engagement Model → People & Capabilities → Performance Management
```

Each feeds the next. Skip prioritization and you waste planning cycles. Skip planning and your
engagement model has no foundation.

## Part 3: Account Prioritization Matrix

### Step 1 — Attractiveness Scoring

For each account, calculate:

| Field | Formula | Description |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| A | Company IT/R&D spend ($M) | IT: 2–3% of revenue; R&D: 8–15% |
| B | Offshorable % | Typically 80% |
| C | Total Offshorable Potential | C = A × B × 0.5 (labor arbitrage factor) |
| D | Currently tapped by captive/low-cost vendors ($M) | |
| E | **Untapped Potential** | E = C − D |

### Step 2 — Ability to Win Scoring (Scale: 1–7)

| Dimension | 1 (Weakest) | 7 (Strongest) |
|-----------|------------|--------------|
| F — Competitive positioning | New entrant | Exclusive / only alternative |
| G — Internal capability | Lead position in <30% services | Lead position in >80% services |
| H — Strength of relationship | Greeting level only | All stakeholders at Total level |
| I — CSAT scores | Low | High |
| **J — Ability to Win** | **J = (F+G+H+I) / 4** | Composite score |

### Competitive Intensity Scale (F)
- 7 = Exclusive or only alternative
- 6 = Front runner with >30% lead
- 5 = Front runner with <30% lead
- 4 = Shared position with competitors
- 3 = Not front runner, >30% gap with leader
- 2 = Not front runner, <30% gap
- 1 = New entrant

### Internal Capability Scale (G)
- 7 = Lead in >80% of services
- 6 = Lead in >70%
- 5 = Lead in >60%
- 4 = Lead in >50%
- 3 = Lead in >40%
- 2 = Lead in >30%
- 1 = Lead in <30%

### Depth of Relationship Levels (H)
| Level | Ability |
|-------|---------|
| Total | Go beyond the transaction; identify and relate in any setting |
| Emotion | Relate to the emotional context of business judgments |
| Feeling | Relate to the rational of business judgments |
| Idea | Know business judgments |
| Fact | Know business details of the contact |
| Greeting | Greet only |

### Strength of Relationship Scale (H)
- 7 = All stakeholders at Total level
- 6 = Majority at Total level
- 5 = Majority at Emotion level
- 4 = Majority at Feeling level
- 3 = Majority at Idea level
- 2 = Majority at Fact level
- 1 = Majority at Greeting level

### Step 3 — Prioritization Matrix (Plot E vs J)

```
          High Attractiveness (E)
    ┌─────────────────┬─────────────────┐
    │  Deepen         │  Pursue         │
    │  Capability     │  Aggressively   │  High Ability
    ├─────────────────┼─────────────────┤  to Win (J)
    │  Pursue         │  Maintain       │
    │  Opportunist.   │  Share          │  Low Ability
    └─────────────────┴─────────────────┘
       Low Attractiveness      High Attractiveness
```

Bubble size = incremental opportunity in $M.

## Part 5: Account Planning — Sweet Spot Identification

Three steps to identify new client opportunities:

**Step 1** — Prioritize functional opportunities at company level (e.g., IT vs. R&D vs. Operations)

**Step 2** — Identify sweet spots from major service lines within prioritized functions
(e.g., SAP within IT, Oracle within Finance)

**Step 3** — Prioritize towers within sweet spots using heat maps
(e.g., CRM implementation within SAP)

**Output:** Attack plan with 4-quadrant prioritization per function/service line

## Part 7: People, Roles & Governance

### Account Manager vs. Sales

| Model | Description | Recommended? |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| Integrated (same person) | Sales + AM combined; deeper relationship but limited scalability | For smaller accounts |
| Distinct (split) | NN handled by Sales; EE/EN handled by AM | **Recommended for Platinum/Gold** |

AM and Delivery Manager should have a single point of contact (SPOC) with clear cross-visibility.

### RAPID Decision Framework

| Role | Description | Rules |
|------|-------------|-------|
| **R** — Recommend | Does 80% of the work; broad visibility | Only one R per decision |
| **A** — Agree | Extraordinary veto (regulatory/legal) | Assign sparingly; A is on the R |
| **P** — Perform | Executes the decision | May be multiple Ps |
| **I** — Input | Provides relevant information that could change the decision | Avoid proliferation |
| **D** — Decide | Final authority | Only one D; locate at right organizational level |

### Governance Authority Matrix (Illustrative)

| Activity | Exec Sponsor | Geo/Vertical Sales | AM/Client Partner | Delivery | Pre-Sales |
|----------|-------------|-------------------|------------------|----------|-----------|

| Develop attack plan | D | I | P/R | I | — |
| Develop account plan/vision | D | A | P/R | I | — |
| Understand client org chart | — | — | P/R | — | — |
| Develop client map | D | I | P/R | I | — |

## Part 9: RFP / Bid Excellence — Four-Component Process

```
01 Opportunity Qualification → 02 Bid Planning & Management → 03 Scoping & Solutioning → 04 Bid Defense
```

### Known Issues in Current RFP Processes

- Bid/no-bid biased toward bidding; does not factor ability to win
- Limited synthesis of client requirements
- Unclear RFP governance; no single bid manager on point
- Bid team often poorly composed (too many seniors, key people missing)
- Win strategy developed in silos; limited integration time
- No succinct executive summary or solution highlights
- High cost-per-RFP; weak bid defense preparation

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Client Hot Button | |
| SS Proposed Solution | |
| Value Proposition to Client | |
| Discriminators (USP) | |
| Proof Point | |
| Elaboration of Proof Point | |
| Result/Performance Achieved | |

**A2 — Win-ability Scorecard**

Two axes scored:

*Opportunity Attractiveness:*
- Scope and client profile
- Size of deal and business case

*SS Ability to Win:*
- Alignment with SS's capability
- Strength of client relationship and access
- Intelligence on competition and relative position

**Governance:** BMG SPOC categorizes the bid, assigns to relevant pursuit team, and forms the preliminary bid team. Campaign/Solution Board assesses and re-calibrates scoring. No-bid decisions escalate to Head of Large Deals / BMG.

## Part 14: Component B — Bid Planning & Management

### Five Sub-Steps

**B1 — Synthesis of Client Requirements**

Capture across eight categories:

| Category | Fields |
|----------|--------|
| Business drivers | Hot buttons / hygiene factors | Linkage to client's vision/IT strategy | Implications for proposal |
| Technical requirements | Same three fields |
| Service delivery model requirements | Same |
| Transition plan requirements | Same |
| Governance/Engagement model requirements | Same |
| Process, methodologies and tools requirements | Same |
| HR and change management requirements | Same |
| Commercial & pricing objectives | Same |

**B2 — Win Strategy**

*Part 1 (Hot Buttons & Hygiene Factors):*
| Hot Button | SS Proposed Strategy (Win Theme) | Key Elements of SS Expertise to Showcase |

*Part 2 (Discriminators):*
| Positive Discriminators | SS Winning Theme Statement | Value Proposition | SS Proposed Approach |

Document for each proposal section: Business Solution, Technical Solution, Service Delivery Model,
Transition Planning, Governance, Process/Methodologies, HR/Change, Commercials/Pricing.

Also capture competitor negative discriminators:
| Competitor | Their Strength or SS Weakness | Gap | SS Approach to Counter |

**B3 — Bid Team Composition & Work Planning**
- Identify expertise; mobilize bid team based on win strategy
- Define GANTT work plan with module owners and deadlines
- Budget: Role | Name | Dedicated/Part-time | Effort (man-days) | Cost per day | Total Cost

**B4 — RFP Scorecard & CEO Dashboard**

Track each active RFP:
| RFP | TCV | Budget ($) | Senior Exec Reviewer | Current Stage | Status | Roadblocks | Resolution | Cost to Date |

Status codes: ✅ On track | ⚠️ Delayed by X days | 🔴 Stalled

**B5 — Proposal Summary & Executive Summary Framework**

Mirrors the Opportunity Analysis template — same fields, but now "Result/Performance Promised"
rather than "Achieved." Drives the overall proposal approach and executive summary.

Benefits statement for client:
| Strategy | Key Expected Benefit + Drivers | Estimated Benefit ($) |
(Separate rows for Financial Benefits, Operational/Process Benefits, Other Benefits)

## Part 15: Component C — Scoping & Solutioning

**C0 — Refine Client Understanding (Live Document)**
- Refreshed synthesis by category (same eight categories as B1)
- Updated after every client interaction, workshop, or advisor touchpoint

**C1 — Client Engagement**
- Engagement plan per workshop: objectives, owners, clarification questions
- Capture consolidated clarification questions by category: question | clarification forum | SS owner

**C2 — Solution Development & Integration**
- Integrated business, technical, and operational solution
- Solution roadmap covering all towers with key client outcomes at each stage

**C3 — Reviews (Three-Stage)**

| Review | Purpose | Reviewers |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| **Blue Team** | Validate win strategy thoroughness, competitive advantage | Exec sponsor(s), LOB heads, Sales AM from same vertical in different geo, LOB nominated expert |
| **Pink Team** | Evaluate proposal approach and solution highlights; test executive summary from client's POV | Exec sponsor, Sales AM, LOB nominated expert, VSG nominated expert |
| **Red Team** | Evaluate full proposal for client focus, completeness, communication of win strategy | Exec sponsor(s), LOB nominated expert, technology architect, VSG expert, CoE/practice experts, HR/BFC, technical writer |

### Review Agendas

**Blue Team (Win Strategy):**
- Opportunity profile and OA scorecard
- Client understanding overview (hot buttons, hygiene factors, business case)
- Win strategy: themes, competitive review, positive/negative discriminators
- High-level value proposition, delivery approach, commercial structure
- Risk mitigation and KSFs

**Pink Team (Proposal Approach):**
- Brief sales cycle history and win strategy summary
- Refined client understanding
- Proposal summary and solution highlights (cross-tower)
- Delivery risks, governance, transition strategy, resourcing
- Revenue profile, cost model, commercial risks
- Sales strategy and client/advisor engagement plan

**Red Team (Proposal):**
- Full proposal review: exec summary, integrated solution map, tower-specific solutions
- Integration dependencies, governance/operating model, HR/transition plan
- Business case and commercial/pricing structure
- Risk and mitigation (commercial, delivery, HR, 3rd party, contractual/legal)
- Articulation: style, tone, language, key messages per section
- Plan for bid defense and client visits

## Part 16: Component D — Bid Defense

**D1 — Bid Defense / Presentations**
- Finalize strategy, approach, and team composition
- Set agenda; script and role-play; execute dry run
- Campaign Board agrees, Solution Board signs off plan

**D2a — Bid Evaluation Questionnaire**
- Send to client post-bid for both successful and unsuccessful bids
- Capture structured feedback for win/loss analysis

**D2b — Post-Bid Analysis**
- Synthesize reasons for win/loss at RFP stage
- Combine client feedback + internal assessment
- Feed into quarterly capability and bid process review

**D3 — Client Visits**
- Understand client's objective for the visit
- Plan SS tour, agenda, scripting, role play, logistics and cultural nuances
- Execute dry run; post-visit debrief and internal evaluation

## Part 17: Complete RFP Checklist

### A — Opportunity Qualification
- [ ] Capture opportunity profile (scope, size, business case, legal)
- [ ] Assess key client objectives and early view on solution/value proposition
- [ ] Complete Win-ability Scorecard (attractiveness + ability to win)
- [ ] Re-calibrate scoring and decide bid / no-bid

### B — Bid Planning & Management
- [ ] Capture and synthesize client understanding (all eight categories)
- [ ] Workshop to define win strategy: hot buttons, hygiene factors, discriminators
- [ ] Blue Team Review on win strategy; incorporate suggestions
- [ ] Workshop to define proposal approach and executive summary ("answer first")
- [ ] Document expected client benefits clearly
- [ ] Pink Team Review on proposal approach and benefits; incorporate suggestions
- [ ] Mobilize appropriate bid team
- [ ] Define work plan: module owners, timelines
- [ ] Set up RFP scorecard; establish ongoing status checkpoints; flag roadblocks
- [ ] Hold bid team debriefing workshop

### C — Scoping & Solutioning
- [ ] Collate clarification questions by category for each client workshop
- [ ] Define objectives, engagement strategy, and team for each workshop
- [ ] Refine client understanding and debrief team after every major interaction
- [ ] Identify reusable collateral from knowledge management portal
- [ ] Create operational and technical solution
- [ ] Integration and synthesis workshop across all towers
- [ ] Iterative refinement with module owners
- [ ] Prepare first integrated draft
- [ ] Refine executive summary, storyline, communication
- [ ] Multiple Red Team reviews with well-defined checklists
- [ ] Senior management review and sign-off

### D — Bid Defense
- [ ] Planning session: finalize bid defense team, set plan, execute dry run
- [ ] Planning session: finalize client visit team, logistics, execute dry run
- [ ] Send bid evaluation questionnaire (successful and unsuccessful bids)
- [ ] Internal assessment incorporating client feedback
